## Engine Mechanics

- **Gigantic creatures**: two-card creatures played as a pair, implemented via `server/game/GiganticCard.js`. Set examples: `server/game/cards/04-MM/Deusillus.js`, `04-MM/NiffleKong.js`, `04-MM/UltraGravitron.js`.
- **Special rarity "Dark Aember Vault"** (7 entries in MM, reused by later sets): the deck-variant marker card coupled with Mass Mutation's mutated-deck scheme.

## Gameplay Notes

- Giant pair-halves change combat math: playing both halves yields oversized creatures, making removal effects (Asura-class damage, purges) proportionally more valuable.
- Variant rarity jumps to 48 in this pack (highest of the FFG-era sets), reflecting the wider deck-generation pool.
- Sanctum returns alongside Saurian, deepening protective/capture playstyles.
