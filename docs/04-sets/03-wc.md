## Engine Context

- Introduces Saurian exalt: converting amber into creature-driven effects, e.g. `server/game/cards/03-WC/CincinnatusRex.js` (optional `ability.actions.exalt()` on fight readies each other friendly card).
- Star Alliance enters with multi-house crew/officer synergies.

## Gameplay Notes

- The set's headline is the house roster change: Saurian and Star Alliance enter; Sanctum rotates out (last appearance until reprints).
- Exalt teaches the trade-off of "spend amber now, get repeatable tempo later," a mechanic later sets counter with capture effects.
- More creature-centric distribution (199 creatures, the most creature-heavy pack to that date) pushes mid-range battleline play.
