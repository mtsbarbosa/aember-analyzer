## Gameplay Notes

- Vault Masters sets are prize/promo-adjacent deck waves; they remix current-rotation houses rather than adding mechanics.
- Enhanced icon cards are deck-fixed: bonus icons are printed on specific copies, not chosen at deck build.
- For analysis: treat VM25 decks as a curated snapshot of the 2025 competitive-strength houses.
