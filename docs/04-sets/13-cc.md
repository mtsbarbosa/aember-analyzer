## Engine Mechanics

- **Archon Powers**: 28 cards typed `archon power` under pseudo-house `archonpower`. These are identity-side abilities for the deck ("standby rules"), not normal playable cards: e.g. Artificer ("You may play artifacts as if they belonged to your active house") or Commanding Presence ("After a player chooses Saurian as their active house, exalt each non-Dinosaur creature").
- Implementation status in keyteki: no engine or card-code references to archon power exist yet (`grep archonpower src` returns nothing), so these are data-only for now. Engine dir `13-CC` contains 56 implementations of the standard new cards.

## Gameplay Notes

- Hobby-retailer sealed-play set: officially legal only against other Crucible Clash decks, so power scaling is tuned for that pool rather than standard play.
- Identity flips: archon powers make your archon card itself an engine piece, converting "who you are" into a first-class mechanic like prophecies did.
- 42 enhanced-icon print variants ride the reprint pool.
