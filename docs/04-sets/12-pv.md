## Engine Mechanics

- **Prophecy**: the set's new card type (22 entries, house `prophecy`). Prophecy cards sit under your identity card; active/facing state flips and "fulfill" on triggers.
- Engine support: `server/game/GameActions/ActivateProphecyAction.js`, `DeactivateProphecyAction.js`, `FlipProphecyAction.js`, `FulfillProphecyAction.js`.
- Card-side verb: `this.prophecyReaction({...})`. Example: `server/game/cards/12-PV/AskAgainLater.js` (names a house at opponent turn start; if the revealed deck-top does not match, `ability.actions.fulfillProphecy` detonates the fulfillment effect).
- 19 enhance-flagged reprints (Dark Minion, Skorpeon, Digitallia, Dark Centurion, Salvay) return as icon-tuned variants.

## Gameplay Notes

- Fate-telling: prophecy cards let you bet on future board events; fulfilling pays a large swing normally worth 2-3 actions of value.
- The "named-house/reveal" pattern (Ask Again Later) can lever your opponent's house choice as a trap, which changes house-selection metagame.
- Redemption support continues from ToC; Brobnar appears only via reprint entries (14).
