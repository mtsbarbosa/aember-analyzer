## Gameplay Notes

- Dragon-paced set: Ouboros leans on resource stacking (hoard) and channeling "enhance" icons into gigantic lines.
- Entrench is the set's new trick: creatures that stay exhausted in your ready step fuel "if this is exhausted" triggers (like Waspscream's takeover) while dodging ready-step removal timing, giving slow engine decks another axis of control.
- Enhanced prints continue the pre-release bonus-icon program first surfaced with AS's 35 enhance entries.
