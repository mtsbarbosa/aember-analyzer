## Engine Mechanics

- **The Tide**: a shared positional mechanic (high/low tide per player). Deck includes a dedicated `the tide` card with `Omni: Raise the tide` (DT.json), and engine support (`isTideHigh`, `raiseTide`, tide messages in `server/game/game.js`). Card usage example: `server/game/cards/05-DT/AllTideUp.js` (gain 1 amber if tide is high, otherwise raise it).
- **Evil Twins**: 49 cards at Evil Twin rarity are dark reflections of creatures from other sets; decks get them as paired alternates implemented as separate classes (e.g. `server/game/cards/05-DT/5c077EvilTwin.js`, `05-DT/MontyBankEvilTwin.js`).
- Note: `GiganticCard` is NOT used in this set (contrary to common assumption); gigantic cards are in `04-MM` and `10-MoMu`.

## Gameplay Notes

- Tide is a shared macro-decision: many cards check `isTideHigh()`, so raising the tide defers payoff one turn; when to raise it (before or after your opponent's key forge) is the set's central timing skill.
- Unfathomable enters with sea/deep-themed creatures and tide synergy.
- Evil twins give decks a secondary personality: the same creature with an inverted or darker play pattern.
