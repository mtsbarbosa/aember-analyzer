# Mechanical and constructed-being traits

Traits on machines and modified beings. Verified against pack extraction.

- **ai** (first AoA; houses: logos, geistoid). Artificial intelligence units.
- **clock** (see 05-artifacts-and-objects.md).
- **cyborg** (first AoA; houses: logos, geistoid, skyborn, brobnar, shadows, ekwidon, ouboros, staralliance). Half-mechanical beings; hooks: `cards/03-WC/CupOfWater.js`, `cards/03-WC/SpontaneousAwakening.js`.
- **mutant** (first AoA; houses: logos, dis, brobnar, sanctum, saurian, skyborn, untamed, staralliance, mars, shadows, redemption). Mutated beings; the most-referenced trait in card code, 34 hook files spanning MM/DT/WoE/VM25/PV/CC, e.g. `cards/04-MM/MutagenicSerum.js`, `cards/06-WoE/MadProphetGizelhart.js`, `cards/07-GR/Krrrzzzaaap.js`, `cards/11-VM25/Purify.js`; typical hooks: gaining/losing the mutant trait, counting mutants.
- **robot** (first AoA; houses: logos, mars, ekwidon, geistoid, brobnar, shadows, skyborn, staralliance, saurian). Mechanical creatures; hooks: `cards/02-AoA/EmpBlast.js`, `cards/03-WC/ChiefEngineerWalls.js`, `cards/03-WC/Mender.js`, `cards/03-WC/SpontaneousAwakening.js`, `cards/03-WC/CupOfWater.js`, `cards/03-WC/SilAT8.js`.
- **tentacle** (first MG24; houses: keyraken). Kraken-limb tag (MG2024); flavor only.
