# Role and vocation traits

Traits describing what a being does. Verified against pack JSON extraction plus grep of `hasTrait(...)` across `server/game/cards`.

- **analyst** (first AS; houses: ekwidon). Data/investment professionals (Ekwidon stock-analysis theme).
- **artisan** (first AS; houses: ekwidon, skyborn, mars). Crafters.
- **assassin** (first AS; houses: skyborn, saurian, shadows). Killers.
- **diplomat** (first AS; houses: ekwidon). Negotiators.
- **hunter** (first AS; houses: skyborn, brobnar). Big-game hunters.
- **knight** (first AoA; houses: brobnar, dis, sanctum, skyborn, ekwidon, redemption). Chivalric warriors; strong hooks: `cards/02-AoA/EpicQuest.js`, `cards/06-WoE/HonorableClaim.js`, `cards/06-WoE/CallToAction.js`, `cards/13-CC/RoundTable.js`, `cards/13-CC/ColonelMariana.js`, `cards/13-CC/SquireRecruitment.js`.
- **leader** (first AS; houses: dis, skyborn, saurian, mars, ouboros, logos, sanctum, shadows, staralliance, untamed, brobnar). Commanders.
- **merchant** (first AoA; houses: sanctum, ekwidon, skyborn). Traders.
- **monk** (first AoA; houses: sanctum, redemption). Ascetics; hooks: `cards/13-CC/CorrosiveMonk.js` and one redemption card.
- **philosopher** (first AS; houses: ekwidon, saurian). Thinkers.
- **pilot** (first AS; houses: ekwidon, skyborn, mars, staralliance). Vehicle operators; hooks in 2 files.
- **pirate** (first AS; houses: skyborn, brobnar). Pirates (Skyborn corsairs).
- **politician** (first CC; houses: saurian, ekwidon, mars, shadows, staralliance, redemption). Lawmakers (CC).
- **priest** (first CotA; houses: sanctum, unfathomable, saurian, redemption). Priests; hook: `cards/07-GR/DeepPriestGlebe.js`.
- **ranger** (first AoA; houses: untamed). Wardens (rangers).
- **recruiter** (first AS; houses: broad, all 12 + geistoid/ouboros). Recruiting abstracts.
- **scientist** (first AoA; houses: logos, mars, ekwidon, shadows, unfathomable, staralliance, brobnar, redemption). Scientists; hooks: `cards/02-AoA/ScientistsBane.js`, `cards/12-PV/Curiosity.js`, `cards/12-PV/RooftopLaboratory.js`.
- **soldier** (first AS; houses: mars, saurian, ekwidon, shadows, redemption). Troops; hook: 1 file.
- **thief** (first AoA; houses: sanctum, shadows, ekwidon, staralliance, redemption). Stealers; hooks: `cards/02-AoA/HeistNight.js`, `cards/03-WC/ThievesBane.js`, `cards/05-DT/Submersible.js` (`hasTrait('thief')`).
- **witch** (first AoA; houses: untamed, redemption). Casters; hooks: `cards/02-AoA/TheWitchingHour.js`, `cards/13-CC/WitchQueen.js`.

Related abstract role tags: **agent**, **ally** (see 01-mars-species.md), **evaluator** and similar do not exist as tags.
