# Discord, curse, and cult traits

Traits tied to Dis corruption and later curse mechanics. Verified against pack extraction.

- **changeling** (first MG24; houses: dis, redemption). Metamorphs (MG2024); hook: `cards/10-MoMu/Picaroon.js` checks `hasTrait('changeling')`.
- **cultist** (first WoE; houses: unfathomable). Deep-cultists (WoE); flavor only.
- **curse** (see 05-artifacts-and-objects.md) - CC curse engines, later 07-GR and 10-MoMu curse engines; card texts count curse cards or mutate their effects.
- **sin** (first CC; houses: dis). The Seven Sins as artifact creatures; hooks: all 7 sin cards themselves (`cards/13-CC/Desire.js`, `Envy.js`, `Gluttony.js`, `Greed.js`, `Pride.js`, `Sloth.js`, `Wrath.js`).
