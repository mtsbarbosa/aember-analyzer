# House-specific and episode traits

Traits tied to a single house or a single episode/set. Verified against pack extraction.

- **adagion** (first AS; houses: skyborn). Skyborn companion entity; part of a larger being. No hooks.
- **cirrus** (first AS; houses: skyborn). Cloud-living sky species. No hooks.
- **cultist** (first WoE; houses: unfathomable). Deep-cultists (WoE). No hooks.
- **getrookya** (first AS; houses: ekwidon, skyborn). Migratory sky species (AS). No hooks.
- **halyard** (first AS; houses: skyborn). Skyborn ship-part tag. No hooks.
- **handuhan** (first DT; houses: staralliance). Star Alliance species (DT). No hooks.
- **ironyx** (see 01-mars-species.md).
- **j'reen** (first AS; houses: skyborn). Skyborn species. No hooks.
- **krxix** (first DT; houses: staralliance). Star Alliance species (DT); hooks in 2 files.
- **niffle** (first CotA; houses: untamed). See 02-humanoid-peoples.md.
- **phoenix** (first WoE; houses: brobnar). Rebirth bird, WoE only. No hooks.
- **proximan** (first DT; houses: staralliance). Star Alliance species (DT). No hooks.
- **raptrix** (first AS; houses: skyborn). Skyborn raptor species. No hooks.
- **stormkin** (first AS; houses: skyborn). Storm-dwelling sky species. No hooks.
- **sylicate** (first MG24; houses: staralliance). Crystal species (MG2024). No hooks.
- **tentacle** (first MG24; houses: keyraken). Kraken-limb tag (MG2024). No hooks.
- **variant** (first PV; houses: brobnar). Variant tag (PV); 1 hook file.
- **getrookya/cirrus/adagion** all belong to the Skyborn (set 15/AS data file) episode species wave.
