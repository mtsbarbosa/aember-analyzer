# Mystical and magical-being traits

Traits on spirits, undead, and psychic entities. Verified against pack extraction.

- **angel** (see 02-humanoid-peoples.md).
- **demon** (first AoA; houses: dis, brobnar, redemption). Infernal beings; one hook file.
- **psion** (first DM; houses: ouboros, brobnar). Psychic beings (DM); flavor only in card texts.
- **shapeshifter** (first AS; houses: geistoid, ekwidon, staralliance, logos). Shape-changers; no direct hooks.
- **specter** (first AS; houses: geistoid, shadows, staralliance, saurian, sanctum, logos, brobnar, mars, ekwidon, unfathomable). Ghosts; hooks: `cards/01-Core/ShadowSelf.js`, `cards/04-MM/Soultender.js`.
- **spirit** (first AoA; houses: sanctum, staralliance, redemption, untamed). Nature/ancestral spirits; no direct hooks.
- **witch** (see 04-roles-and-vocations.md).
