# Human-aligned and humanoid peoples

Traits on humans and related culture-folk. Verified against pack JSON extraction.

- **angel** (first AoA; houses: sanctum, redemption). Divine servant beings; no direct `hasTrait` hooks but Sanctum cards reference them in texts.
- **bräkken** (first CC; houses: brobnar). Sheepfolk of Brobnar. Hook: `cards/07-GR/Hallafest.js` checks `hasTrait('bräkken')`.
- **cat** (first CC; houses: untamed). Felines; pair with niffle theming. Hook: 1 file (`hasTrait('cat')` in a Niffle-adjacent card).
- **cyborg** (first AoA; houses: logos, geistoid, skyborn, brobnar, shadows, ekwidon, ouboros, staralliance). Half-mechanical people. Hooks: `cards/03-WC/CupOfWater.js` and `cards/03-WC/SpontaneousAwakening.js` check `hasTrait('cyborg')`.
- **elf** (first AoA; houses: shadows, untamed, skyborn, staralliance). Elves. No direct `hasTrait('elf')` hooks; flavor only.
- **faerie** (first AoA; houses: untamed, geistoid, shadows). Fairy-folk; 1 hook.
- **fisher** (first DT; houses: untamed). River-folk of the Crucible (DT only).
- **giant** (first AoA; houses: brobnar, ekwidon, redemption, shadows). Large folk; 6 hook files including `cards/02-AoA/Drummernaut.js`, `cards/06-WoE/StiltKin.js`, `GiantsBane.js`, `Beanstalk.js`.
- **goblin** (first AoA; houses: brobnar, untamed). Classic goblin race; no direct hooks.
- **horseman** (first CC; houses: sanctum). The Four Horsemen (Sanctum); hooks: 2 Sanctum cards check `hasTrait('horseman')`.
- **human** (first AoA; houses: brobnar, logos, sanctum, shadows, untamed, dis, ekwidon, skyborn, mars, staralliance, saurian, redemption). Humans; hooks: `cards/02-AoA/HumansBane.js`, `cards/05-DT/QyxxlyxPlagueMaster.js`, `cards/05-DT/SacrificialAltar.js`.
- **imp** (first AoA; houses: dis, brobnar). Small devils; no direct hooks.
- **niffle** (first CotA; houses: untamed). Niffle beast-folk; 4 hook files: `NiffleQueen.js` (buffs niffle), `TroopCall.js`, `NiffleKong.js`, `NiffleBrute.js`.
- **wolf** (first MG24; houses: untamed). Wolves; hooks: 2 files including a ToC and a DM-era wolf synergy card.
